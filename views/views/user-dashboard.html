Emergency Types

Select your emergency type to see immediate first aid steps:

Heart Attack Emergency Steps

1
Call emergency services immediately
2
Have the person sit down and rest
3
Loosen any tight clothing
4
If conscious, give aspirin to chew (if available)
5
Be prepared to perform CPR if they become unconscious

Road Accident Emergency Steps

1
Ensure your own safety first
2
Call emergency services immediately
3
Don't move injured persons unless absolutely necessary
4
Stop any severe bleeding with clean cloth pressure
5
Keep injured persons warm and calm until help arrives

Breathing Difficulty Emergency Steps

1
Call emergency services immediately
2
Help the person sit upright
3
Loosen any tight clothing around neck/chest
4
If asthma, help them use their inhaler
5
Stay calm and reassure the person

Severe Bleeding Emergency Steps

1
Call emergency services immediately
2
Apply direct pressure with clean cloth
3
Elevate the injured area if possible
4
Don't remove soaked cloth - add more on top
5
Keep the person warm and still

Burns Emergency Steps

1
Call emergency services for serious burns
2
Cool the burn with cool (not cold) running water
3
Remove jewelry or tight items before swelling
4
Cover loosely with sterile non-stick dressing
5
Don't apply creams or break blisters

Bone Fracture Emergency Steps

1
Call emergency services immediately
2
Don't move the injured area
3
Apply ice wrapped in cloth to reduce swelling
4
If open wound, cover with clean dressing
5
Keep the person warm and still until help arrives
